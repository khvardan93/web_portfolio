Բakyi Domino

Personal project — demo playable below

A domino game inspired by the courtyard domino tables of Armenia, with six traditional variants: Telephone (the default), Kozel, Classic Kozel, Sea Goat, General and Chechevitsa, each a rule class behind a shared interface. Online rooms run on Photon Fusion 2 Shared Mode with a deterministic lockstep session: players agree on the deal through a SHA-256 commit-reveal seed so nobody can rig the shuffle, every move carries a state hash to catch desyncs, and bots take over seats when someone leaves. Zenject wires the layers together, the UI is localized in Armenian, English and Russian, and 180 automated tests cover rules, game flow, online and UI.
